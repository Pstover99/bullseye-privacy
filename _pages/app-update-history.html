Bullseye Ballistics

App Update History

v1.3.12

Wind Corridor

  • Full-value crosswind at a glance. NET WIND now shows only the crosswind that actually moves your bullet, and which way it pushes. Headwind and tailwind no longer inflate the number.
  • Net Wind & Direction. The total wind speed and clock direction now sit at the top of the Corridor Summary, alongside Advanced Ballistics, Simple average and Firing line only.
  • “Why different?” explains itself. See where the wind is along your shot, how much each stretch counts in each method, and plain-English reasons based on the live wind.
  • One-tap Capture. Point at the target and tap Capture to lock your GPS, direction of fire and your firing-line Calypso ultrasonic meter's direction in one step.
  • Clear prompts, no guessing. If your firing-line meter needs its direction, the map shows it greyed out with “Needs Capture”, and a tap tells you exactly what to do. After a restart, a banner reminds you to Capture.
  • Smarter meter placement. Recommended meter spots are based on the real bullet path, with clearance shown over both the ground and the treetops.

v1.3.11

Wind Corridor

  • Tap NET WIND for the full picture. It opens the Corridor Summary right from the map.
  • Picks up where you left off. Your target distance and meter calibration are saved. After a restart, just confirm your saved calibration instead of redoing it.
  • Make it look your way. New controls cover:
    • particle density and speed
    • gust swirl
    • terrain-shaped wind
    • sensor markers
    • satellite photo dimming

Solve and Sensors

  • Know why you're on manual wind. The Wind card tells you which meter isn't connected, and offers a one-tap switch to the Wind Corridor or another connected wind meter.
  • Tell identical sensors apart. Two sensors with the same name now show the last four characters of their ID, and the app flags it when you pair a second one.
  • Backed up with your settings. Corridor appearance, your saved target distance and meter calibration are included in your settings backup.

v1.3.9

  • Net wind is weighted for your active saved load and saved with each logged shot for truing.
  • Optional USGS satellite and terrain map layers.
  • Export recordings without GPS location.
