Bullseye Ballistics

App Update History

v1.3.9

  • Wind Corridor: connect a Calypso LoRa receiver and downrange wind meters to see live wind along your line of fire.
  • New views: See the Wind (live wind flow), Path Profile, and Meter Placement.
  • 1 m LiDAR, where available, profiles your entire path's elevation and shows the ground and tree height along your line of fire to suggest where to set your meters.
  • Try Wind Corridor anytime in Demo mode.
  • Net wind is weighted for your active saved load and saved with each logged shot for truing.
  • Optional USGS satellite and terrain map layers.
  • Export recordings without GPS location.

v1.3.8

  • Match stages have a Coach button. One tap opens Sagitta with a plan to prepare for and shoot the stage.
  • When coaching a stage, Sagitta reads its target numbers in step order.

v1.3.7

  • Home tile status text uses the full tile width, paired tiles match heights, and long titles like Cloud Backup wrap.

v1.3.6

  • Set or clear one direction of fire for a whole match stage.
  • Text fits without clipping or overlap on Home, Solution, HUD, Matches, Rifles, Shot Log, Chrono, Load Dev, Groups, Sensors, and Sagitta, including at larger font sizes.
  • Home tiles show their full status, Chrono's buttons stay pinned on screen, and shot numbers up to 999 fit.
  • In landscape, Sagitta's header stays clear of 3-button navigation. HUD chrono readings keep their spacing, and rifle stat tiles work as buttons with screen readers.

v1.3.5

  • Logged shots record your rifle's pre-recoil cant from a connected external display. It shows in shot details and CSV exports.
  • Save your rifle's cant at zero in Rifle Setup by typing it or capturing it from your external display. It shows on logged shots and in CSV exports.
  • In Matches, type, edit, or clear a step's direction of fire in degrees (true or magnetic north).

v1.3.4

  • Logged shots save your rifle's zero and split windage into wind, spin drift, Coriolis, zero offset, and aero jump.
  • CSV exports add shot angle, chronograph velocity, powder temperature, station pressure, clean bore, barrel round count, your zero, and the windage breakdown.

v1.3.3

  • Gun number works for subsonic and rimfire loads out to 600 yd/m. Mil gun numbers under 2 mph round to 0.1 mph.
  • Hunting Zero and Hunt mode add a Small game preset (2 in vitals) and show point-blank range separately from energy and expansion limits.
  • Target HUD adds an Adaptive MV switch, your own stat order, and a clear label when no chrono reading is in use.
  • In velocity checks, edit barrel round counts and import faster from long chrono lists.

v1.3.2

  • Pair an Athlon Velocity Pro chronograph in Sensors and record live strings in Chrono mode.
  • With Premium, each chrono reading on the Solution screen logs a shot and asks for your call.
  • With Premium, Adaptive MV adjusts muzzle velocity from today's readings.
  • Import chrono strings or logged shots into velocity checks and workup rungs.
  • Truing uses your measured velocities.
  • Hit rates leave out shots with no call and show how many were left out.

v1.3.1

  • Voice shot calls stay accurate through long sessions and catch the first word of a call made right after the last one.
  • Voice shot calls log only what you said: no guessed directions, and everyday words don't count as calls.
  • If the phone falls behind, voice shot calls catch up on their own.

v1.3.0

  • Sagitta uses the accent color you choose in Settings.
  • Voice shot calls stay accurate and reliable across long sessions.
  • Cloud backup and Sagitta keep working when another phone runs a newer version of Bullseye.
  • Smaller download.
