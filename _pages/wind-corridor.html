Calypso LoRa wind meters

Wind Corridor

Pair Bullseye Ballistics with Calypso LoRa wind meters set out downrange, and for the first time you can actually see the wind between you and the target.

Each meter reports the wind where it stands, and the app joins the readings into one live picture of the whole corridor, from the firing line to the target. The solver uses that same wind at every point along the bullet's path.

Learn to read it

See what the wind looks like

Pick a meter at 900 yards and read its speed and direction. Then look at the mirage, the leaves, the trees and the grass near 900 yards. That is what that wind looks like at that distance.

Do it often enough and you learn to read the wind from the mirage, leaves, trees and grass alone, at any distance and on any range, with or without meters.

On the screen

The whole corridor, live

  • Moving wind from the firing line to the target, faster where the wind is stronger.
  • Every meter at its distance, with its speed, an arrow and its clock direction.
  • Stretches no meter covers marked as assumed, so you know what is measured and what is not.
  • A meter that drops out shows No signal and is left out until it is back.
