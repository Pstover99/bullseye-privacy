How it works

More than a calculator

Bullseye Ballistics doesn't just solve trajectories. It's built around a feedback loop.

01

Measure conditions

Sensors fill in wind, temperature, pressure and humidity. GPS and the compass fill in latitude and direction to target.

02

Generate the solution

Elevation, a bracketed wind hold, hit probability, time of flight, and velocity and energy on target.

03

Take the shot

Read the dial on the phone or on the HUD on your rifle. Call it with a tap, or say it and stay in the rifle.

04

Record the impact

Hit or miss, where it landed, the distance, the time, and the conditions at that moment.

05

Analyze trends

Hit rate by distance, where your misses land, cold bore against warm, and dope by density altitude.

06

Improve future solutions

True the load from your own log with one tap. The next solution starts from what your rifle really did.

It moves beyond a traditional ballistic calculator and closer to a complete precision rifle data collection and performance analysis platform.

And it's only getting smarter from here.

Less typing

Let the sensors do the typing

Our goal is to capture as many inputs as possible automatically and keep manual input to a minimum. The more sensors you have, the less time you spend futzing with your solver, or pointing your phone in a particular direction like a cave person before you shoot. You enter the bare minimum as conditions, stages and situations change through the day.

What goes in
  • Muzzle velocity, and its change with powder temperature
  • Bullet BC and drag model
  • Zero offsets and zero conditions
  • Sight height and twist rate
  • Temperature, pressure and humidity, live from your sensors
  • Wind speed and direction, live from your wind meter
  • Latitude and direction to target, from the phone's GPS and compass
  • Distance and shot angle
What comes out
  • Elevation correction
  • Wind hold, bracketed
  • Hit probability on the target you pick
  • Stability factor and time of flight
  • Velocity and energy, including on target
  • Spin drift, aerodynamic jump and Coriolis, rolled into the dial
  • Where the bullet goes transonic and subsonic
  • The conditions it was solved for
In the app

Large, bright, clean

Built to read at the range. Pick a feature to see its screen, or tap a screen to see it full size.

What it does

Everything for the shot, and after it

Set up a rifle and load once. Bullseye solves the shot with a full point-mass engine, then keeps a record of what actually happened.

Solve

Firing solutions

A point-mass solver with G1 and G7 drag, in MOA or mil. Spin drift, aerodynamic jump and Coriolis are worked out for you and rolled into the final firing solution.

Sense

Live sensors

A constant, real-time feed from several Bluetooth sensors at once, merged per reading. Stale readings are released automatically, so the solution never runs on an old number.

Odds

Hit probability

Your hit chance on the target you pick, with a ranked error budget. Set the wind bracket you're holding for and the card tells you when the live wind matches.

True

Truing

Two-point truing to match your observed dope, or a fit over the misses in your log. One tap applies it.

Log

Voice shot calls

After the shot, say "hit" or "miss, three tenths mil left". The app captures and logs it, with the atmospherics and wind at that moment. Your hands stay on the rifle.

Measure

Group analysis

Measure a group from a photo. The app plots every shot and shows the group's size, shape, direction and center, then what it does on any target at any distance.

Develop

Load development

Run and track charge and seating ladders, and keep every result. Let the app do the math and build out the rungs from your step size.

Compete

Matches

Stages and targets in engagement order. Step through them from the solution screen with no distances to type on the clock.

Maintain

Barrel life and cleaning

Track the barrel's whole life by actual round count against expected. Log routine and deep cleans, and get a reminder when the next one is due.

Adjust

Powder temperature

Muzzle velocity by powder temperature, from a table you keep per load. The solution follows the ammo temperature on the day.

Display

External HUD

Pair an M5StickS3 and the live solution streams to a small display on your rifle. Choose what it shows and in what order.

Keep

Cloud backup

Optional. Sign in and your rifles, shot log, groups and matches are backed up. Target photos and sensor pairings stay on the phone.
