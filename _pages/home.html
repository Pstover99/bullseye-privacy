What it does

Everything for the shot, and after it

Build a profile once. Bullseye solves the shot with a full point-mass engine and keeps a record of what actually happened.

Solve

Firing solutions

Elevation and windage in MOA or mil, from G1 or G7 drag. Spin drift is applied for you. Coriolis uses your GPS latitude and a compass bearing.

Sense

Live sensors

Connect several Bluetooth sensors at once. Wind, temperature, pressure and humidity feed one live solution.

True

Two-point truing

Enter what you really dialed at two known distances. Bullseye adjusts muzzle velocity and drag to match your rifle.

Log

Shot log and voice calls

Say "hit" or "miss, one mil low" and the shot is logged. Track your hit rate by distance.

Measure

Groups from a photo

Photograph the target, then mark two reference points, your aim point and the holes. Bullseye measures the group and how far its center is from your aim.

Develop

Load development

Record charge ladders and seating tests with velocity and group size per rung. Turn the best rung into a load.

Compete

Matches

Build the stage list, then step through it on the day. Each target's dial is ready, and every shot you log is tagged with its stage.

Maintain

Barrel life and cleaning

Rounds count as you log shots. See barrel life left, and turn on a reminder for when a clean is due.

Adjust

Powder temperature

Enter your load's velocity at two or more temperatures. The solution follows the ammo temperature on the day.

In the app

Works with

Use more than one at a time. Pick which sensor supplies each reading.

Private by design

Your data stays yours

  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything works without an account. Only sign-in and cloud backup use the internet.
  • Cloud backup is optional and off until you sign in. Android's own phone backup may also keep a copy.
  • Target photos and voice audio never leave your phone.
