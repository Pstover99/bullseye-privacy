01 / 16Solution screen

The dial, large and bright

Elevation and windage in MOA or mil, with the linear value in inches or centimeters under each. One tap switches MOA and mil on every screen that dials.

  • Hit probability against the target you choose, with the error budget ranked: wind read, muzzle velocity, range estimate, ballistic coefficient and group dispersion.
  • Wind band. Set the bracket you are holding for and the card says when the live wind is inside it. A readiness line says when your conditions are met.
  • Call the shot with Hit, Miss or No-call. A 3×3 grid takes the miss direction with one tap, and you can add how far off in mil or MOA. Or say it: voice calls are built in.

  • Shot strings started, named and continued from the solution screen. The running hit count stays in view.
  • Stats you choose. Velocity and energy at the target, time of flight, stability factor, wind, aerodynamic jump and conditions. Turn each on or off and set the order.
  • Target presets for hit probability: MOA and mil sizes, steel plates, IPSC zones, popper, deer and elk vitals, or your own size.
  • Every input is marked Live or Manual, so you know where each number came from.
  • Full-screen card with large text for reading at the rifle. The dial scrubs distance on the clock, or captures an azimuth for one distance and moving targets.
  • Full brightness while the solution is open, and a keep-screen-on option.

02 / 16Wind, conditions and sensors

Type as little as possible

The more sensors you connect, the less you type. Bullseye stays connected to several at once and fills the solution from them.

  • Several sensors at once, merged per reading. A reading that goes stale is released on its own, so the solution never runs on an old number.
  • Live feed from the Calypso Ultrasonic, Calypso WeatherDot and WeatherFlow Tempest, plus RuuviTag and most sensors that use the standard Environmental Sensing service.
  • Pick the source for wind, temperature, pressure and humidity. If that sensor drops out, the reading goes back to manual rather than silently switching.
Show 5 moreShow fewer
  • Wind smoothing, fixed or by distance band, with a staleness threshold.
  • Calypso direction calibration, so the meter can face any way and does not need to be moved.
  • Auto fill of the solution from Bluetooth, and auto-reconnect to saved sensors.
  • GPS latitude and compass azimuth from the phone, with a GPS power profile you choose. The live heading shows its accuracy, and you can lock it once set up.
  • Battery and connection state for every device on the sensors screen.
03 / 16Ballistic solver

A full point-mass engine

Bullseye traces the bullet's flight in small steps with RK4 integration, checking the rate of change four times in every step. Gravity, drag, wind, spin and the Earth's rotation all act on the bullet as they change along the way, so the result is accurate past the transonic zone, not just near the zero.

  • G1 and G7 drag models.
  • Drop, windage, velocity, energy, stability and time of flight at any range, with a full trajectory table. Transonic and subsonic ranges are highlighted.
  • Spin drift, aerodynamic jump and stability factor are calculated and applied.
Show 8 moreShow fewer
  • Coriolis from latitude and azimuth, with an option to turn it on at launch.
  • Shot angle from the phone's sensors or typed by hand.
  • Temperature, station pressure and humidity go into the solution, and the density altitude is shown.
  • Several wind readings blended into one solution, by clock position or bearing.
  • Two-point truing to match what you really dialed, plus a fit over the misses in your log.
  • Muzzle velocity by powder temperature, from a table you keep per load.
  • Cold-bore and clean-bore offsets for the first shot, when you say the first shot is one.
  • Zero conditions stored per load, so the zero corrects when the day is different.

04 / 16Coriolis, spin drift and jump

The small corrections, done for you

The Earth turns under the bullet while it flies. Shooting north or south moves the impact mostly up or down; shooting east or west moves it mostly sideways. The longer the flight, the bigger the shift. At 1,000 yards it is usually a few tenths of a mil, depending on your latitude and direction of fire, and it grows from there.

Bullseye takes your latitude from GPS and your direction of fire from the compass, works out the Coriolis correction, adds spin drift and aerodynamic jump, and folds all three into the dial. You can also type the latitude and azimuth by hand.

05 / 16Rifles and loads

Set it up once

Any number of rifles, each with its own loads. Pick the current rifle and load once and the whole app uses them.

  • Rifle: name, bore diameter, twist rate and direction, sight height.
  • Load: bullet weight, diameter and length, drag model and ballistic coefficient, muzzle velocity, zero range, zero conditions, maximum range and range step.
  • Advanced, per load: cold-bore and clean-bore shots, zero offsets, zero conditions, muzzle velocity against temperature, and truing. Most shooters can skip these.

  • Preview the full trajectory for the load before you shoot it.

06 / 16Shot log and analysis

A record that pays you back

Every call you make is a shot in the log, with the distance, the solution the app gave you, and the conditions at that moment. That is a real-world record of your rifle, not a paper DOPE card.

  • Shot strings with each shot's call, miss location, distance, name, notes and where each number came from.
  • Analysis across the strings you pick: hit rate by distance, where your misses land, and cold bore against warm bore.
  • Dope by density altitude, and predicted against observed for each shot.
Show 3 moreShow fewer
  • Sensor payoff: how much the live sensors improved the solution over typing it in.
  • Truing from your log: a muzzle velocity offset and drag scale fitted to your logged misses, applied with one tap.
  • CSV export of any string.
07 / 16Groups

Measure it, then see what it means

Record a group three ways: measured from a photo of the target, entered shot by shot, or entered as the numbers you already have.

  • From a photo: camera or gallery, a known distance on the target for scale, perspective correction, then place each hole under a fixed crosshair and nudge it.
  • The numbers: extreme spread, mean radius, spread on each axis with a 95 percent confidence interval, and how far the group center sits from your aim point. In inches, centimeters, MOA or mil.
  • The plot: one and two sigma ellipses, scale rings, the aim point and every hole drawn at caliber, so you see the group's size, shape, direction and center.
Show 4 moreShow fewer
  • Pooled per rifle and load. The pooled dispersion feeds hit probability on the solution screen.
  • Simulate: put a saved group on any target preset at any distance, drawn to shape, with a shot cloud colored by hit and miss. Drag the aim point and read the offset. Rifle only, or with today's conditions.
  • Export the target image with the group's statistics drawn on it.
  • The list shows thumbnails, with multi-select and batch delete.

08 / 16Matches

Walk it once, then shoot it

  • Stages and targets in engagement order, with a size and label per target and a briefing per stage.
  • Match run: step through the stages and targets from the solution screen. Each target's dial is ready, and there are no distances to type on the clock.
  • Every shot you log during the run is tagged with its stage and target.
  • Details you choose to keep per stage: time limit, round count and position. Changes save as you go.
09 / 16Voice shot calls

Stay in the rifle

Optional and off by default. After the shot, say "hit", or "miss, three tenths left", and Bullseye logs it with the solution and the wind and weather at that moment. Your hands stay on the rifle.

  • What it understands: hit, impact, miss and no call, a direction, and how far off in mil or MOA. "Undo" or "scratch that" takes the last shot back.
  • A follow-up window for adding the direction after the call.
  • On-device recognition. Audio never leaves the phone and is never written to a file.
Show 2 moreShow fewer
  • Shots are not calls. Impulse blanking and a near-field gate keep the shot itself, and the shooter next to you, from being heard as a call.
  • Listens only while a solution is on screen, and the settings page says exactly what it listens for and where the audio goes.
10 / 16External HUD

The dial on the rifle

Pair an M5StickS3 as a Bullseye HUD and the live solution streams to it. Mount it where you can see it from behind the scope and the phone can stay in your pocket.

  • Choose what it shows and in what order: range, elevation, windage, velocity, time of flight, wind, hit probability and the display's own battery. A preview shows the layout before you send it.
  • Brightness and dial size are set from the phone.
  • Paired on the sensors screen like any other device, with its link state and battery.

Sign in is for backup only

Everything works offline

The solver, the sensors, the log, groups, matches and the HUD all run with no connection. Sign-in and cloud backup are the only parts of the app that use the internet.

14 / 16Barrel life, cleaning and reminders

Know where the barrel is

  • Round count per barrel, counted from the shots you log, against the life you expect. Add rounds you fired without logging.
  • Cleaning intervals by rounds or by days, whichever comes first, for routine and deep cleans.
  • Log each clean with what was done: chamber, bolt, bore, muzzle device. Shots after a bore clean are tagged clean-bore and kept out of truing.
  • Reminders on the phone when a clean is due or the barrel nears its life, plus one-off reminders such as a clean before a match. Off until you turn them on.
