Delete data

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Bullseye Ballistics is developed by Core Bridge, LLC. You can delete some or all of your data and keep your account. To delete the account and everything backed up to it, see Delete your account.

Delete single records in the app

Delete a rifle, load, shot string, shot, group, match, workup, velocity check or cleaning record from its own screen. With cloud backup on, the delete reaches your backup within about a minute when you're online, or the next time the app syncs, and removes the record's contents from it.

If backup is off, or your Premium has ended, when you delete, the backed-up copy stays until backup runs again.

For each deleted record, a small marker stays in your backup: the record's ID and when it was deleted, so your other phones learn about the delete. It holds nothing else, and it is removed when you delete your account.

Delete Sagitta conversations

  • One conversation: in the chat, open the menu (three dots) and tap Delete this conversation.
  • All of them: open Settings → Sagitta → Delete all conversations.

Either one removes the conversations from your phone and from your backup. Sagitta's server never keeps your conversations. Google, which runs the AI model for us, may keep a copy for up to 24 hours, or up to 90 days if its automated checks flag a question for review. We can't delete those copies.

Stop backing up

Open Settings → Account and backup → Open cloud backup and turn off Back up to the cloud. This stops new backups. What is already backed up stays in your account until you delete it.

What is kept while you have an account

These stay until you delete your account, unless a shorter time is given.

  • Your account: your email address, your account ID, and the hashed password or the record of the Google account you signed in with.
  • Your Sagitta plan: Google Play's record of the plan you bought, so the plan keeps working.
  • Sagitta's usage count: how much of each period's allowance you used.
  • Answers you report: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Files you send Sagitta: a PDF is deleted as soon as Sagitta has read it, and always within a day. We don't store photos.
  • Your Terms acknowledgements: which version of the Terms you accepted and when, with your email address. These are kept even after you delete your account, as proof the Terms were accepted.
