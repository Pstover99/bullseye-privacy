How it works

Set it and forget it

Several sensors at once

Connect a wind meter and a weather sensor together. You choose which sensor supplies each reading, and a new sensor fills in whatever the others don't cover.

Fills in your numbers

With Auto Bluetooth fill on, live readings go straight into your solution. Turn it off and your sensors stay connected without changing your numbers.

Never an old number

If a sensor stops reporting, its reading is marked stale and dropped from the solution, so you never shoot on wind from a minute ago.

Reconnects on its own

If a sensor drops out, Bullseye reconnects to it when it comes back.

Wind as a clock position

Set your wind meter's facing once with your phone's compass, and live wind direction becomes a clock position against your direction of fire.

Steady holds in gusty wind

Live wind is averaged before it reaches your hold, more at long range, so one gust doesn't swing your solution. See how wind smoothing works.
