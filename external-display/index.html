External display

Bullseye Ballistics External Display

Unique to Bullseye Ballistics is an optional companion device which can be mounted to your rifle or scope. It displays only the pertinent information you need available to you during a trigger press and is configurable to show/hide the info based on your preferences.

The External Display has built-in level and angle sensors and communicates with the Bullseye Ballistics app in real time.

Try the simulator
On the display

Information displayed

Not optional
  • Elevation hold
  • Windage hold
  • Level
  • Angle
Optional
  • Range/Distance
  • Time of flight
  • Velocity on target
  • Wind
  • Hit probability
  • Battery percentage

YOU control the display. If you need the text to be larger or brighter, use the app to adjust it to your liking. You can also control the range/distance using the external display buttons.

The built-in level only appears if it detects the rifle is canted left or right and visually assists your re-leveling. Once re-leveled, it stops complaining and returns to displaying whatever information you selected to have displayed (time of flight, wind, hit probability, etc.). The holds always remain displayed regardless of the level status.

Scope mount

Mount it on your scope

The scope mount clamps to your scope tube and holds the External Display off the side, next to the turrets. Shift your eye, read the display, and you're back behind the glass without breaking position.

Check that you're level, read your elevation and windage holds, and double-check the wind before you press the trigger.

Position

Stay on the gun

No lifting your head and no reaching for a device. The display sits where a quick glance finds it, so you can keep your phone in your pocket.

At a glance

Level, holds and wind

Your holds are always on screen. The level bar appears when the rifle is canted, and the wind info shows if enabled.

Secure

Held snugly in place

The display inserts securely into the sleeve and lips on the sleeve hold it in. A washer on the back adds a magnetic hold. With or without the washer, it stays put even upside down.
