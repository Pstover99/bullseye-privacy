Pro-Grade Ballistic Solver

Everything you expect from a serious solver.
Firing SolutionsElevation and windage in MIL or MOA for any distance, from a precision-grade trajectory engine. It models drag (G1 or G7) as it changes with Mach number, accurately tracking the bullet through the transonic zone, along with air density, spin drift, aerodynamic jump, Coriolis and shot angle, the same physics serious long-range shooters rely on.
Computational Fluid Dynamics, for the RangeEngineers use computational fluid dynamics to map how air moves through a space. Wind Corridor brings that idea to long range shooting. Calypso LoRa meters placed downrange feed a live wind field from the firing line to the target, and the solver applies the wind at every point along the bullet's path. Powerful education tool for learning to read the wind.
Adaptive Wind SmoothingAverages gusty wind readings, with more smoothing at longer distances.
Unlimited Profiles, Unlimited RangeSolve at any distance, no range cap. Unlimited rifle profiles.
Coriolis and Shot AngleCorrects for the earth's rotation (latitude from GPS) and for uphill or downhill shots.
Capture AzimuthRecords the compass direction you're facing for the Coriolis correction with one tap.
Match Builder from Photo or File (Done for you)Photograph or upload a match book and Sagitta AI builds the stages and targets for you. More on Matches.
Hit ProbabilityShows your chance of a hit on the target you choose.
Stability CalculatorGyroscopic stability (Sg) for a saved load or any bullet, with the twist rate needed.
Gun Number CalculatorThe wind speed that moves your bullet 0.1 mil per 100 yards, checked against a full solve at each range.
Miss Pattern AnalysisA heat map of miss directions with a plain-language diagnosis of the likely cause.

Your Rifle, Your Load

Velocity, zero and bore data that match your real rifle.
Rifle and Load ProfilesMultiple loads per rifle, with copy, rename and one active setup.
Zero OffsetsCorrects for a zero that hits above, below, left or right of the aim point.
Zero ConditionsRecords the weather on your zero day, typed in or filled from sensors, so solutions correct for today's conditions.
Hunting ZeroHunting Zero works out exactly how high to sight in for your load, the animal's vital zone, your own group size and the conditions where you hunt, so you can hold dead center out to your maximum point-blank range. A color-coded hold guide shows where to hold beyond it.
Hunt ModeA solver mode built for the field. Inside your max point blank range it simply says Hold Center, past it you get the exact hold, and every solve is checked for an ethical shot: enough energy on target and reliable bullet expansion. More on Hunt Mode.
MV vs TemperatureA temperature-to-velocity table that adjusts muzzle velocity for powder temperature.
Velocity ChecksPaste or type chronograph readings to save sessions with average, SD and ES.
MV Spread and Barrel Wear AdjustmentUses your measured velocity spread in hit probability and adjusts MV as the barrel wears.
Cold-Bore Velocity PromptAsks before each solve whether this is a cold-bore shot and uses your cold-bore velocity for that shot only.
Clean-Bore TrackingUses a separate velocity for the first shots after cleaning until the barrel settles.
Handload RecordsStores powder charge, seating length and CBTO or COAL with each load.

Live Sensors and Display

Connect multiple sensors. Live wind, weather, level (cant), shot angle and your holds on a screen by your scope.
Live Weather and Wind SensorsCalypso Ultrasonic, Calypso WeatherDot and WeatherFlow Tempest feed live readings into every solve.
Choose Which Sensor Supplies Each ReadingPick the source for wind, temperature, pressure, humidity and angle, or type any of them in.
Adaptive Wind SmoothingAverages gusty wind readings, with more smoothing at longer distances.
Live WindLive Wind shows what the wind from your Calypso wind meter is doing, live: a wind clock with the swing over the last two minutes, a speed bar with the High, Average and Low, and a hold for the wind right now and for each of them.
External Display (HUD)A small screen near your scope that shows your holds.
Bullseye Ballistics External Display (HUD)Bullseye Ballistics External Display goes beyond showing holds. Its built-in level only appears when your rifle is canted left or right more than 0.5 degrees. Another built-in sensor sends the shot angle to the solver in real time, where it's used for calculating holds and stored with the shot logs. Its buttons change the target distance so you can re-solve without touching the phone. It also shows range, angle, velocity, time of flight, wind, hit probability and battery, with your choice of fields, three dial sizes and adjustable brightness.

Know Your Odds

See your chance of a hit before you break the shot.
Hit ProbabilityShows your chance of a hit on the target you choose.
Miss BreakdownSplits the miss risk by cause (wind, range, velocity, BC, shooter) and names your biggest error.
Wind Readiness IndicatorA wind-band marker on the solution showing whether conditions are ready to shoot.
Group MeasurementSimple group measurement from photo.
Bullseye Ballistics Group MeasurementGoes well beyond measuring size. Each group is saved to its rifle and load and can be measured from a photo, plotted shot by shot or entered as stats in inches, MOA or mil. Group dispersion can feed straight into the hit probability calculations on every firing solution. Simulate overlays your group on a chosen target and shows your hit chance as you slide the distance out. Ask Sagitta reviews the group for you.
Group Hit SimulationShows your chance of hitting a chosen target with your group as distance increases.

Sagitta AI

An AI ballistician that reads your own data.
AI AssistantBasic AI chat bot answers questions.
AI - Ballistician ModeSagitta's Ballistician mode analyzes your real-world data (logged shots, atmospherics at time of shot, wind readings vs solver holds at time of shot, miss patterns, velocity checks, groups and load workups) and makes recommendations, such as your biggest error source at distance, which ladder rung to choose, or whether your load needs truing. Prefer a different voice? Switch to Plain for straight answers, Caveman for the short version, or Sassy for answers with attitude.
