Lesson 8 and the top section on the live page describe it as "4.0 mph 7:30", crosswind and tailwind together. The combined figure has moved to the Corridor card's new "Net Wind/Direction" row, for example "2.6 mph · 8:00".

- **"Why different?" is much longer now:**

- **The wind is stronger at the bench:** about 9.5 mph from 9 o'clock. The page shows about 7 mph.
- **Settings have moved.** There's no separate "Units & Filter" sheet, no "Path & Placement Settings" with shooter height, and no "Wind Meters" tab. The gear opens "Visualization & Recording", and the tabs are See the Wind / Corridor.
