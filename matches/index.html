How it works

From match book to first shot

Upload

Give Sagitta the match book PDF, or a photo of one stage card.

Sagitta builds it

Stages, positions, targets, distances, sizes and the course of fire, in shooting order.

Check it

Each stage shows its steps and whether they add up to max points. Fix anything with a tap.

Shoot it

Start a stage and tap Next after each shot. The solver already has the next distance.

Sagitta AI points out when the math doesn't math and clarity is required from the MD (happens sometimes, no big deal). You should still walk the course either way, and while doing so, capture the DOF at the stages so the solver can bake in Coriolis to your solves (optional, but a little exercise won't kill you).

Your matches

Every match, ready to shoot

  • Set up from a match book opens Sagitta. Take a photo or choose the PDF the MD sent you.
  • Each match shows its stage and target count. The one you're shooting is marked Running, with the stage and target you're on.
  • Open a match to see its stages in shooting order, each with its time, round count, starting position and distance range.
  • Drag a stage to reorder it. Changes save as you go.
Inside a stage

Everything about a stage, on one screen

Tap a stage to see how it's shot. Stage 1, Pillars: two positions, five targets from each, ten shots in 90 seconds.

Repeats and rules

Spelled out, shot by shot

  • Repeats become steps. Platform is T1 to T3 three times from the top of the spool, so it's nine steps, in order.
  • Stage rules cover the rest: points per hit, a round limit, ending on the first miss, the shooter's choice of position and moving targets.
  • Read stage card rebuilds one stage from a photo of the sheet at the bay, when the MD changes it on match day.
  • Edit anything. Tap a step, target or position to change it.
At the line

Tap Next. The distance is already set.

  • Shoot stage starts the run. The solution screen picks up the match.
  • The run card shows the stage description, the step you're on, its target, distance and size.
  • Next target and Previous move through the course of fire. Tap any stage or step to jump to it.
  • Save each target's direction from its position once, and the run uses it from then on.
On the solution card

Shot, Next, shot

The arrows on the full-screen card step to the next target and the dial updates. Hit or Miss logs the shot, tagged with its stage and target.
