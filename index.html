How it works

A loop, not a lookup

A calculator gives you a number. Bullseye gives you the number, then watches what the bullet did and uses it next time.

01

Measure

Sensors fill in wind, temperature, pressure and humidity. GPS and the compass fill in latitude and direction.

02

Solve

Elevation, wind hold, hit chance, time of flight, and velocity and energy at the target.

03

Shoot

Read the dial on the phone or on the HUD on your scope. Call the shot by voice or with a tap.

04

Record

Each shot is saved with its call, where it landed, and the conditions at that moment.

05

Analyze

Hit rate by distance, where your misses land, dope by density altitude, and what the sensors were worth.

06

Improve

True the load from your log with one tap. The next solution uses it.

What it does

Everything for the shot, and after it

Set up a rifle and load once. Bullseye solves the shot with a full point-mass engine and keeps a record of what actually happened.

Solve

Firing solutions

Elevation and windage in MOA or mil, from G1 or G7 drag. Spin drift and aerodynamic jump are applied for you. Coriolis uses your GPS latitude and a compass bearing.

Sense

Live sensors

Connect several Bluetooth sensors at once. Wind, temperature, pressure and humidity feed one live solution, and a stale reading drops out on its own.

Odds

Hit probability

Your chance of a hit on the target you pick, with the biggest sources of error ranked. Set a wind band and the card says when the live wind is inside it.

True

Truing

Enter what you really dialed at two distances, or let Bullseye fit muzzle velocity and drag to the misses in your log. One tap applies it.

Log

Shot log and voice calls

Say "hit" or "miss, three tenths left" and the shot is logged with the solution and the conditions. Or tap the miss on a 3×3 grid.

Measure

Groups from a photo

Photograph the target and mark the holes. Bullseye measures the group, plots it, and shows what it does on any target at any distance.

Develop

Load development

Charge ladders and seating tests with velocity and group size per rung. Type the first, last and step, and the rungs fill in. Turn the best rung into a load.

Compete

Matches

Build the stages and targets, then step through them from the solution screen. No distances to type on the clock.

Maintain

Barrel life and cleaning

Rounds count as you log shots. See barrel life left, log cleanings, and get a reminder when a clean is due.

Adjust

Powder temperature

Enter your load's velocity at two or more temperatures. The solution follows the ammo temperature on the day.

Display

External HUD

Pair an M5StickS3 and the live solution streams to a small display on your rifle. Choose what it shows and in what order.

Keep

Cloud backup

Optional. Sign in and your rifles, shot log, groups and matches are backed up. Photos, location and pairings stay on the phone.

In the app

Built to read at the range

Big numbers and high contrast. Full brightness while the solution is open, so it reads in the sun.

Bluetooth

Works with

  • Calypso Ultrasonic wind meter, for wind speed and direction
  • Calypso WeatherDot
  • WeatherFlow Tempest shooting weather meter
  • RuuviTag
  • Most Bluetooth sensors that use the standard Environmental Sensing service
  • M5StickS3 as an external HUD

Use more than one at a time. Pick which sensor supplies each reading.

Private by design

Your data stays yours

  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything works without an account. Only sign-in and cloud backup use the internet.
  • Cloud backup is optional and off until you sign in. Android's own phone backup may also keep a copy.
  • Target photos and voice audio never leave your phone.
